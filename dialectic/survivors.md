# survivors.md — claims that withstood Round 4

Inclusion requires: attacked by at least two agents with non-overlapping commitments, and surviving in a form the attackers could not further reduce. A survivor may be *smaller* than the claim that entered.

---

## S-01 — Pearl blankets vs. Friston blankets is Lukács's reification thesis, independently derived

Bruineberg, Dołęga, Dewhurst & Baltieri distinguish the Markov blanket as a construct *inside a model* from the claim that it demarcates a *real boundary in the world*, and show the second does not follow from the first. Lukács defines reification as social relations taking the form of relations between things. These are the same error, reached from analytic philosophy of science and from Western Marxism with no shared premises.

**Why this matters more than a coincidence:** it means the anti-reification constraint on the programme is *not* a soft humanistic caution that a sufficiently careful modeller can discharge. It is a claim about inference that the formal literature independently confirms. Anyone asking "where exactly is the Markov blanket of empire?" is asking for a Friston blanket, and the answer is that the question presupposes what must be shown.

**Converted question:** not *where is the blanket* but *which conditional independences actually hold, over which variables, for how long, and who is enforcing them*. That is answerable, and the answer is usually "few, briefly, and by specific actors" — which is a sociological finding, not an ontological one.

## S-02 — Bayesian model selection requires a fixed evidence functional; Aufhebung is the transformation of the functional

The FEP theorist and the Hegel scholar arrived at this from opposite motives — one defending mathematical precision, one defending Hegel from flattening — and produced the same conclusion.

Model comparison presupposes a single scoring functional `F` under which candidate models are commensurable. In the *Phenomenology*, consciousness supplies its own criterion, and when a shape fails, **the criterion changes with the object**. A formalism with a fixed `F` cannot represent that; it is not a matter of needing a richer model space.

**This is the cleanest incompatibility found in the cycle.** It is not terminological, not a matter of levels of description, and not resolvable by more computation. It says exactly where the analogy breaks and why.

**Constructive residue:** `F` should be treated as a variable in any study of institutional or scientific change — observable as documented standards of adequacy (`formalizations.md` F-04). That is a research instruction, not a synthesis.

## S-03 — Colonization is a change in coordination *mechanism*, not in agent-internal states

The whole hypothesis set `H1`–`H6` misses Habermas's concept, because all six transform something inside a single agent. Colonization is the replacement of coordination-by-redeemable-validity-claims with coordination-by-steering-media in symbolic-reproduction domains.

**Operational criterion that survived the empirical sociologist's attack:** at a decision site, can the affected party raise an objection the deciding party is *obliged to answer in reasons*, and are the reasons of a type they can contest?

**Sharpest instance:** a score is not contestable in kind. Appeal is procedural recourse; it is not argument. Deploying unarguable scores in domains of symbolic reproduction is `R → S` substitution, and it leaves records.

This also dissolves a persistent confusion: **legibility (Scott) and colonization (Habermas) are different mechanisms with different remedies.** Uniform categories with intact reason-giving is one pathology; unarguable decisions within familiar categories is another. Most critiques of algorithmic governance conflate them, and the conflation explains why "explainability" is offered as a remedy for problems it cannot touch.

## S-04 — `Lebenswelt = generative model` fails by over-determination

Refuted by phenomenology (anonymity and pre-givenness; the individual subscript is already wrong), by Habermas (three reproduction processes, not a representation), by FEP itself (no specifiable observation space, latent states, or likelihood), and by history of science (no shared conceptual ancestry — Friston's lineage is Helmholtz → Ashby → Pearl → variational Bayes).

Four independent refutations with no shared premise. In an argumentative process without data, over-determination is the strongest evidence obtainable.

---

## S-05 — Historical transformation of the intelligible voids FEP's own preconditions

FEP proper requires a nonequilibrium steady state over a **fixed** state space. The programme's central phenomenon is `Ω → Ω'`. Either the transformation is contained in a pre-given super-space `Ω*` — so nothing historically novel can occur — or it is genuine and FEP has no formalism for it.

Structure learning does not escape this: Bayesian model reduction and expansion move within a fixed space of possible models.

**This is not a critique of FEP.** It is a statement of its domain. FEP-style modelling is legitimate *intra-epochally*. The epoch boundaries must be identified by non-FEP means, and no FEP model may claim to describe the crossing.

## S-06 — The pre-given total state space is Absolute Knowing in measure-theoretic disguise

**The one concept the collision produced that none of the input frameworks possessed.**

Horn (i) of S-05 — a space `Ω*` containing every state that will ever be intelligible — is, in content, Hegel's Absolute: the totality within which all determinations are already positioned. But where Hegel asserts it as a philosophical thesis and thereby exposes it to critique, the formalism installs it as a **technical precondition**, where it is invisible: it looks like a modelling convention, and no reviewer objects to a measurable space.

Adorno's identity-thinking is subsumption under a fixed conceptual scheme. A σ-algebra is a fixed conceptual scheme *with a completeness axiom*. The non-identical, in this setting, is precisely what has no measurable set.

**Consequence for the programme:** the deepest ideological commitment of a formal social model is not in its priors, its objective function, or its policy space. It is in the **declaration of the state space**, which is made before modelling begins, is rarely argued for, and cannot be revised by evidence gathered inside the model. *That is where critique should aim.*

This survived attack by the FEP theorist ("this is just a domain restriction") — because the point is not that the restriction is illegitimate but that it is *normatively loaded and structurally unmarked*.

## S-07 — "Imperial" is a predicate of a relation, not of a collectivity

The source paper's own best move, strengthened. Its differentia:

**A relation between collectivities `A` and `B` is imperial when (i) `A` can compel `B`'s states to be reported in `Ω_A`; (ii) `B` cannot compel the converse; (iii) the residue of `B` not expressible in `Ω_A` is rendered administratively nonexistent rather than merely unrecorded.**

Clause (iii) is what distinguishes empire from ordinary asymmetric translation, and it is measurable — residual-category load, torque between biography and record, and the treatment of the unclassifiable as absent rather than as unknown.

This makes "is the corporation a super-agent?" the wrong question and dissolves the question the source paper found it could not answer. Nothing needs to *be* an empire for a relation to be imperial. **The ontological question was malformed** (a Round-4 model-space transformation, outcome C).

---

## S-08 — The programme has been run before, and Habermas's concept was its refutation

FEP's genuine near-ancestor is cybernetics — Ashby's requisite variety, Conant & Ashby's good-regulator theorem, Deutsch, Beer — not German idealism. Cybernetic social theory culminated in Luhmann's autopoietic systems theory. Habermas's system/lifeworld distinction and the colonization thesis were formulated **specifically against Luhmann** (the 1971 exchange).

**Therefore:** a "critical computational sociology" grounded in FEP risks reconstructing Luhmann while flying a Habermasian flag. The Frankfurt-school vocabulary would then name the position being occupied rather than the position being defended.

This is not fatal to the programme, but it is a hard constraint: **any FEP-based social model must state what prevents it from collapsing into functionalist systems theory.** If the answer is "we will be careful," there is no answer. The credible answers so far are S-09 and F-08 — locate the critical function outside the model, and measure coordination mechanisms rather than system functions.

## S-09 — The immanent critique of a classificatory system is its contestation record, not a better model of it

From `contradictions.md` K-006, and the only exit found from Lukács's objection that the modeller occupies the contemplative stance.

No model can be its own critique; reflexivity performed by the observer about the observer is still observation. But the classified are not in the contemplative position — they are in the position of being subsumed, and their refusals, appeals, misclassification complaints, and workarounds constitute an *immanent* record of where the categories fail by the system's own standards.

**Redirection:** the primary critical data are appeal records, residual-category growth, front-line discretion and its documentation, torque between biography and record, and refusal. Model diagnostics are secondary. This answers the source paper's question — *what does an immanent critique of AI look like using the tools of AI?* — with: **not primarily by building a better model, but by instrumenting the contestation surface, which is a thing AI systems could be built to preserve and currently are built to minimise.**

That last clause is the design consequence, and it is actionable.

## S-10 — Incommensurability is not alterity; it is a middle-range phenomenon of sustained contact

Contextuality requires contexts large enough to constrain, overlapping enough to conflict, and small enough that no single context contains everything. Both degenerate limits read zero: **total mutual opacity** (no overlaps) and **complete assimilation** (one context) are formally non-contextual.

The sheaf formalism therefore cannot represent the wholly other — and this is a delimitation, not a defect. What it models is the incommensurability *internal to translation*, which is precisely where Viveiros de Castro's controlled equivocation lives. Equivocation is a phenomenon of contact, not of isolation.

Neither anthropology nor the mathematics had this distinction in this form. Attacked and unbroken.

## S-11 — Empire coarsens the cover; the obstruction vanishes and the loss relocates

Imposing a single administrative ontology *is* the declaration that all questions are jointly askable. Under that declaration the mathematics reports perfect consistency: nothing contradicts anything, because there is one context and everything in it agrees.

The loss does not disappear. It moves into residual-category load, torque, and the growth of "other."

> **F-06 (contextuality) and F-05 (residuality) are anti-correlated detectors across the imperial process. Neither alone can see the transition between regimes, which is the event of interest.**

Falsifiable, non-obvious, and derivable from neither Scott nor Viveiros de Castro alone. → `formalizations.md` F-09.

## S-12 — Normativity enters at the partition; the formalism is innocent because the guilt was discharged upstream `[ANSWER TO Q-05]`

Draw the Markov blanket around the plantation: a homeostatic system minimising free energy, persisting, well-adapted. Draw it around the enslaved person: a system under catastrophic sustained surprise. **Both descriptions are formally correct and the formalism is indifferent between them.**

FEP does not naturalise domination by endorsing it. It naturalises domination **by making the partition look like a discovery rather than a decision.**

Generalised across four technically distinct objects reached by four routes:

| route | declared object | what it declares |
|---|---|---|
| S-06 | measurable space `Ω` | what can be a state |
| Q-03 / F-06 | cover `𝓜` | which questions are jointly askable |
| K-006 / F-10 | carving `κ` | how situations become cases |
| Q-05 | partition `(μ,s,a,η)` | which system is the one that persists |

Each is declared before the formalism begins, is unrevisable by evidence the formalism generates, carries the normative content, and is invisible because it presents as technical setup.

> **Husserl's *Substruktion* is not Galileo's one-time error. It is the recurring structural moment of every formalisation, and it is locatable: find the objects that must exist before the first equation can be written.**

**The critique procedure this yields, applicable to any formal social model:** (1) enumerate what had to be declared before modelling could begin; (2) identify who declared it, under what authority; (3) ask what evidence the model could generate that would revise it — usually none; (4) ask whether it is contestable by those it binds — usually no; (5) ask what has no place in it, and where that residue goes.

Immanent in the strict sense: it uses only the model's own requirement that it declare a frame. No external standpoint, no rival model.

**Correlation objection recorded, not dismissed** (`meta_critique.md` MC-01): four routes generated by one process may converge because the process prefers a punchline. Partial answer — the four objects are technically distinct with different mathematical roles, and the convergence is at the level of epistemic *position*, not content. Unanswerable part — this finding is this process's own frame declaration becoming visible to itself.

## S-13 — Token-contestation vs. type-contestation; level-2 colonization

- **token-contestation**: objecting to a category's *application* to a case. Inside the carving. F-08's `R`.
- **type-contestation**: objecting to the *category*, with power to force revision of `κ`. On the carving.

Every recourse mechanism in algorithmic governance is token-level — credit disputes, moderation appeals, benefits determinations, data-subject rights. **None contests the taxonomy.**

> **Level-1 colonization: decisions become unarguable. Level-2 colonization: the categories become unarguable — not because they are defended, but because no forum exists in which category-revision is a possible move with institutional uptake.**

AI systems are paradigmatically level-2: the ontology sits in a schema or a label definition, and there is no channel through which the affected population can raise a validity claim about the schema.

This is the second-order form of F-08, and Habermas's own architecture already contains it as discourse *about* norms versus action *under* norms. → `formalizations.md` F-10.

## S-14 — FEP is natively heteronomous; self-evidencing is the formal inverse of self-relating negativity `[THE INVERSION]`

Two attacks that Cycle 1 never faced were constructed and answered in Cycle 2.

**Does hierarchical predictive processing rescue the fixed-functional problem (S-02)?** No. The hierarchy modulates lower-level precision, but the whole hierarchy is scored by one free energy. **The hierarchy pushes the fixed functional upward; it does not eliminate it.** There is no level above the top. Hegel's claim concerns the top.

Residue more valuable than the survival:
- **Heteronomous criterion revision** — a criterion revised from above or outside — available in FEP at every non-top level.
- **Autonomous criterion revision** — a criterion transformed by the failure of the shape employing it — available at no level.

> **A subject whose criteria are revised from above is the colonised subject. FEP models this natively, precisely, and well. It models its overcoming not at all.**

The same conclusion from Friston's own central concept: **self-evidencing** is self-relating, but *conservatively* — persist as what you are. Hegel's self-relating negativity destroys what a thing is through the working-out of its own determination.

> **Self-evidencing is the formal opposite of self-relating negativity. One preserves identity through change; the other destroys identity through its own determinacy. They have been mistaken for each other because both are called "self-relating."**

**Constructive consequence — the framework is preserved by becoming the description of its opposite.** FEP is not a candidate foundation for critical social theory. It is a candidate **formal model of heteronomy** — of what critical theory opposes. Used that way it is precise, mathematically respectable, and immune to the naturalisation charge, because naturalisation is only a danger when a formalism is offered as a description of the good order.

**Not FEP as the science of society. FEP as the exact mathematics of the administered life.**

## S-15 — Enforced coarsening destroys kinds, not details

**Evidence:** `cycle3_reading_the_residue.md`, Punjab print register 1910–1912, n=4,502.

The register saw every item and described each accurately. What it could not do was **aggregate**. There is no predicate in its `Ω` under which 174,000 copies of the Sikh Hand Bill Committee's numbered campaign fall together — the series is split across three topics, and its publisher fragments into five strings. The record survives; the kind does not.

**This corrects F-05's weak form.** Residual *load* is a count of how often a carving fails. What matters is **loss of aggregability**: whether objects that belong together in the world are separable within the scheme.

**Corollary — the sharpest formulation the three cycles produced.** Power operates on aggregates: counts, trends, kinds, threats. A classification that individuates perfectly and aggregates wrongly yields an apparatus that **sees everything and knows nothing.** Empire did not simplify here. It described in detail, at scale, for seventy-five years, and could not name what it was watching.

This is a better account of imperial epistemic failure than "empire simplifies," and it was not available from Scott, from Bowker & Star, or from anything in Cycles 1–2.

**EXTENSION, confirmed longitudinally** (`cycle3_handbill_trace.md`, 10,214 pages, 1867–1942). Loss of kind is not only synchronic. **The register's own genre-vocabulary drifts.** The Punjab Religious Book Society ran a numbered series of short evangelical tracts continuously from at least 1881 to at least 1922; the register called it *"monthly hand-bill, No. 148"* in 1893, listed it by publisher alone in 1908, and called it *"pamphlet No. 3"* — numbering restarted — in 1922. **No query on any single description recovers the series. Only the publisher string does, and that is itself variant.**

> A residual category is a failure of **extension**. Genre-word drift is a failure of **identity through time**. They are different failures, and only the second is invisible to a single-year analysis.

**SECOND-ORDER INSTANCE, found by control rather than inspection.** Four volumes of the run — 1876–80, 1894–99, 1900–03, 1904–06, **1,755 pages, 17.2% of the corpus** — have **no OCR text layer at all**. They are invisible to every kind-level query ever run against this archive, and nothing in the filenames, page counts or documentation announces it. **Empire's register was coarsened once by its topic scheme and again, a century later, by a digitisation pipeline.** The digitisation individuates perfectly (every page imaged) and fails to aggregate. S-15 holds of the archive of the archive.

## S-16 — The residue is structured and recoverable; surveillant thoroughness is the condition of its own critique

Cycle 2 left open whether loss relocated into residuals is *recoverable* or merely *countable*. **Recoverable.** The 967 `Miscellaneous` entries decompose into eleven modern print genres — polemic/reform tracts (19.9%), associational and corporate documents (13.2%), textbooks, clerical manuals, almanacs, price lists — and the uncovered remainder is more of the same. **Every cluster is a modern print genre; not one is a literature.**

The detail survives **because the apparatus was descriptive rather than enumerative.** An extractive register recording only counts would leave nothing to read back.

> **Q-17's surveillant arm moves from *consistent with* to *supported by*.** Surveillant coarsening documents its own classificatory failure in detail, and that documentation is what later makes it contestable. **The register's thoroughness is the condition of using its own product against its categories.**

Immanent critique in the strict sense, executed rather than proposed — and a worked answer to the source paper's closing question. Not a better model of the apparatus. **The apparatus's own record, read against its own scheme.**

*Caveat retained: the 61% cluster coverage is an upper bound, fitted and measured on the same data. The concentration results (§3 of the source file) use one fixed classifier across all topics and are not so vulnerable.*

## S-17 — A scheme can name doctrine and fail to name reform

Corpus-wide placement, one fixed classifier applied identically to every topic:
- **doctrinal dispute → Religion 41.9%, Miscellaneous 14.4%**
- **social reform → Miscellaneous 38.5%, Religion 9.0%**

Social-reform print is **4.3× more likely to be classed as residue than as Religion.** The register could hold a controversy about the Prophet or the Vedas; it had no place for a tract on widow remarriage, female education, caste, or temperance.

**This is not a coverage gap. It is a statement about what the apparatus took the colonised to possess:** doctrines, poems and law — not a reform politics. The carving encodes an anthropology, and the anthropology is legible only from where the carving fails.

Generalises beyond the case: **a classification scheme's residual category is the most informative single object in it**, because the named categories record what the makers expected and only the residue records what they met.

## S-18 — The residual category is the largest thing in the register

**21.5% of entries. 27.1% of all registered copies — 1,882,720, more than Poetry (25.3%) on 30% fewer entries.** Medians are 1,000 throughout (a printing convention); the whole difference is in the upper tail.

The category the apparatus used for what did not fit was, by volume of paper actually printed, **the biggest thing in Punjab print.**

This is the empirical form of S-06: the frame declaration is not a technical convenience with marginal consequences. **Here it misplaced the plurality of the object it existed to observe.**

## S-19 — A category a scheme forbids reappears in its descriptive vocabulary

**Evidence:** `cycle3_q18_genre_vocabulary.md`. 8,459 OCR pages, 1867–1942, two controls passed.

The Punjab register has fields for language, topic, printer, publisher, city, copies, price, format, method. **It has no field for religious community** — the most consequential fact about Punjabi print in the period, and one the Reporter plainly knew, since the glosses are saturated with it.

The classification could not enter `Ω`. It entered `Σ` as a lexical habit:

- `tract` — **81% Christian (1881–84) → 97% Sikh (1907–09)**, Christian to zero, against a background never above 30% Sikh
- `risala` — cross-communal in the 1880s → **85–100% Muslim after 1913**, Christian and Sikh at literal zero
- `hand-bill` — 60% Christian (1881–84) → **100% Sikh (1910–12)**
- `pamphlet` — rises 16 → 210 occurrences and **tracks background**: the administrative word filling the space

**Controls are what make this a result, not a pattern.** Null words (`edition`, `price`) track background exactly in every volume — ruling out the section-position confound, since the catalog is sectioned by language and Punjabi sections are Sikh-heavy. And tightening the context window from ±260 to ±80 characters **sharpens** the effect (tract 94%→97%, risala 87%→100%) while leaving null words unchanged. A neighbouring-entry artefact would weaken.

**Two mechanisms, separated by where the word lives.** `risala` and `hand-bill` occur overwhelmingly in *titles* — publishers' self-naming. `tract`, `pamphlet`, `booklet` occur overwhelmingly in *glosses* — the Reporter's own words. And `tract`, which shows the total handover, is a word the publishers largely did not use of themselves. **The register's describer developed a lexical habit that sorted communities, using a word of his own.**

> **`Ω` and `Σ` stand in compensatory relation. What the categorical layer forbids, the semantic layer carries.**

This gives K-004's prohibition on bundling an empirical mechanism, and it yields a **general method**: to find what a classification scheme suppresses, do not audit its categories — a suppressed category is by definition absent. **Measure the drift of the descriptive vocabulary against a null-word baseline.** The suppressed distinction appears as lexical stratification with no categorical correlate.

**Transfers directly** to any corpus pairing a structured schema with free-text human description — administrative archives, clinical records, annotated ML training sets. A label taxonomy omitting a distinction its annotators find salient should show the same signature. **That is a runnable audit for what a labelling scheme forbids its annotators to say.**

**Consequence for C-18(iv).** The imperial frame does not only present as discovery rather than decision. **It is reactive**: denied a category, its describers improvised one, and the improvised category tracked the communal contest in the print market — in a register with no way to record that it had moved. A third mode alongside extractive and surveillant coarsening: **categories stable on paper, usage drifting underneath them, unrecorded.**

## What did not need FEP

S-01, S-03, S-05, S-06, S-07, S-08 and S-09 are all statements about FEP's limits or about mechanisms drawn from Habermas, Scott, Bowker & Star, Hacking and Lukács. **The programme's surviving content is critical-theoretic and STS-theoretic; FEP's surviving role is as a source of precise negative results about what cannot be formalised the way the source paper hoped.**

That is a genuine contribution and it is not the one the source paper was seeking.
