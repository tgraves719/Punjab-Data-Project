# cycle1_rounds.md — round-by-round record

Focal proposition **Q0**: *Is the Lebenswelt usefully formalizable as a generative model, a possibility-space/horizon constraining generative models, neither, or something requiring a fundamentally different formal ontology?*

---

# ROUND 0 — independent position formation

Each agent `E_i = (O_i, A_i, C_i, M_i, F_i, U_i)`: ontology, assumptions, defensible claims, mechanism, falsification conditions, untranslatable remainder.

## E1 — Active Inference / FEP theorist

`O`: random dynamical systems at NESS; densities over states; conditional independence.
`A`: a generative model is `p(o,s|θ,m)` with typed observation and latent spaces; a Markov blanket is a conditional-independence structure, not a membrane; FEP ≠ active inference ≠ predictive coding.
`C` **ESTABLISHED**: (i) `Lebenswelt = generative model` is not a mathematical statement — no `o`, no `s`, no likelihood is specified; (ii) FEP requires a fixed state space with a stationary density; (iii) structure learning operates within a fixed model super-space, so it cannot represent genuinely novel intelligibility.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: precision/salience `Γ` is the one FEP-native quantity with a plausible social reading, because precision-weighting is well defined and has behavioural signatures.
`C` **INVALID**: `Markov blanket = social boundary`; `prediction error = contradiction`; `free-energy minimisation = sublation`.
`M`: none proposed for the social case — declines to supply one.
`F`: if a social model specifies `o`, `s`, `θ` and a likelihood and makes a prediction that fails, that model is falsified. FEP itself, as a principle, is not falsified by anything, which is why it must not be cited as evidence.
`U`: "meaning," "validity," "recognition" — no FEP-native referents.

**Opening statement, recorded verbatim:** *"The mathematics I am here to defend does not license any of these applications, and saying so is the most useful thing I can do."*

## E2 — Complex systems / ABM theorist

`O`: agents, states, interaction topology, update rules, scheduling, observables.
`C` **ESTABLISHED**: the Lebenswelt as described admits no agent set, no state, no update rule, no observable — it is not underspecified, it is unspecified; "super-agent" is legitimate only as a claim about informational closure of a *named* macro-variable, testable via causal-state or `Ψ`-style criteria; institutions typically show closure in narrow channels only.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: the intervention loop `M_t → A_t → X_{t+1} → D_{t+1} → M_{t+1}` is implementable and would produce non-trivial dynamics.
`C` **INVALID**: treating `(Ω, Π, Σ, Γ)` as one object across four timescales.
`F`: a simulation whose qualitative behaviour is invariant to the choice of `Ω` is not modelling `Ω`.
`U`: normative content of any kind.
**Standing question:** *what would have to be represented in code for this to mean anything?* — asked of every claim; answered for F-05, F-07, F-08; unanswered for everything FEP-derived.

## E3 — Husserlian phenomenologist

`O`: intentionality, horizon, constitution, pre-givenness, intersubjectivity, generativity.
`C` **ESTABLISHED**: the lifeworld is anonymous and pre-individuated — `L_i` is already a category error; horizon has *und-so-weiter* structure and no members, so `Ω` cannot be it; `Ω` presupposes individuation, which horizon is what explains, so the formalisation is circular; **the *Krisis* argument is a prediction about this project** — the *Ideenkleid* thrown over the lifeworld and mistaken for it.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: *Typik* (pre-predicative typification) is the component nearest to a formal treatment, and *Heimwelt/Fremdwelt* (Steinbock) supplies a plurality that is communal and generational, not individual.
`C` **INVALID**: `Lebenswelt = generative model`; blanket-as-Cartesian-boundary.
`F`: the position would be weakened if a formalism made a *phenomenologically recognisable* distinction that phenomenological description had missed.
`U`: horizon, pre-givenness, anonymity, lived body. Not "hard to formalise" — formalising them changes the subject.
**Exercises its permission:** the proposed translation commits a category error at the identification `L = generative model`, and at the point where the lifeworld receives an individual index.

## E4 — Hegel scholar

`O`: determinate negation, self-relating negativity, recognition, objective spirit.
`C` **ESTABLISHED**: thesis–antithesis–synthesis is not Hegel (Mueller 1958); contradiction is structural and ungraded, prediction error is metric and graded; **in the *Phenomenology* the criterion changes with the object** — Bayesian model selection requires a fixed evidence functional and therefore cannot represent this; Geist is a normative achievement sustained by recognition, not a super-organism, and Hegel attacked organicism about the state.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: treating the evaluative functional `F_t` as a variable is the only construction with any dialectical content, and it remains external.
`C` **INVALID**: `M_i → M_{i+1}` as Aufhebung; §339 as support for Markov blankets.
`F`: would revise if shown a formalism where the *criterion* of adequacy is endogenously transformed by the failures it diagnoses.
`U`: *für uns* / the retrospective constitution of necessity; the difference between a change and a *development*.

## E5 — Frankfurt School / Habermas specialist

`O`: communicative vs. instrumental rationality; system vs. lifeworld; validity claims.
`C` **ESTABLISHED**: Habermas's Lebenswelt is de-transcendentalised and is three reproduction processes (culture, society, personality), not a space or a model; colonization is the substitution of media-steering for communicative coordination **in symbolic-reproduction domains** — a claim about a coordination mechanism between actors; **none of `H1`–`H6` is this claim**; Lukács's reification is historically specific and its subjective correlate is the contemplative observer's stance.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: `H8` (mechanism substitution at a decision site) is operationalisable via reason-giving obligations and contestability.
`C` **INVALID**: "lifeworld" as a synonym for culture; the system/lifeworld boundary as a fixed measurable territory (Fraser).
`F`: if sites with high media-steering in symbolic domains show none of the predicted reproduction pathologies, the thesis fails.
`U`: the *achievement* of mutual understanding as against the *form* of the obligation to give reasons. `H8` measures only the latter, permanently.
**Self-directed warning:** the demand that this be modelled computationally is itself a candidate instance of instrumental rationality colonising the analysis.

## E6 — Empirical sociologist

`C` **ESTABLISHED**: "lifeworld," "super-agent" and "colonization" as used in the source paper have no operational definitions; every claim about Palantir has a competing explanation (capacity vs. transformation) that predicts the same observables; "the corporation as agent" contradicts the coalition model of the firm.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: reason-giving and contestability are codable from administrative records; residual-category load is available in nearly every administrative dataset.
`F`: if independent coders cannot reliably code `R` vs. `S` at decision sites, `H8` is not an empirical construct.
**Standing question:** *what evidence would distinguish this from the ordinary institutional explanation?* — answered for F-05, F-07, F-08; unanswered elsewhere.

## E7 — Anthropologist / STS

`C` **ESTABLISHED**: `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B` — different lifeworlds may not be different distributions over a shared space; state spaces encode culturally particular carvings while presenting as neutral; classification produces residual categories, and violence concentrates there (Bowker & Star); legibility destroys *mētis* (Scott); statistical categories are constitutive, not merely descriptive (Desrosières, Hacking).
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: translation loss and residuality are directly measurable.
`C` **INVALID**: any `φ_{B→A}` presented as a well-defined total function — **a well-defined translation is the eradication of the equivocation, which is the imperial act performed as method** (Viveiros de Castro).
`U`: the alienworld as *accessible only as inaccessible*; partial connection without common measure (Strathern).

## E8 — Philosopher / historian of science

`C` **ESTABLISHED**: "Markov blanket" is Pearl's Bayes-net construct (1988), with no continental ancestry; FEP's genuine lineage is Helmholtz → Ashby / Conant & Ashby → Pearl → variational Bayes, i.e. **cybernetics, not German idealism**; the §339 citation is retrospective similarity presented as ancestry; **cybernetic social theory culminated in Luhmann, and Habermas's system/lifeworld distinction was formulated against Luhmann (1971)** — so the proposed programme risks reconstructing the position its critical vocabulary was invented to defeat.
`C` **INVALID**: the Kant→Helmholtz→Friston / Kant→Hegel→Frankfurt convergence as evidence of conceptual kinship. Shared citation is not shared concept.

## E9 — Critical mathematical modeler

`C` **ESTABLISHED**: the declaration of the state space is the most theoretically loaded and least examined act in any social model; a formalisation with no falsification condition is decoration.
`C` **SPECULATIVE**: sheaf-theoretic contextuality is the right *shape* for `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B` because it reproduces the actual phenomenon — pairwise translation succeeds, global reconciliation fails.
`C` **INVALID**: F-01, F-03.
**Self-directed warning:** contextuality may be the anthropologist's objection in more elegant clothing. Registered in advance as K-009, not discovered afterwards.

## E10 — Red team

Strongest case, stated without hedging:
1. Every substantive claim in the programme is carried by Habermas, Scott, Bowker & Star, Hacking or Lukács. FEP adds vocabulary and no predictions. **Marginal explanatory content: zero.**
2. The apparent convergences arise from vocabulary generality. "Boundary," "model," "update," "contradiction" are general enough that any two frameworks using them will appear to align. The afterimage example (D-05) shows the mechanism in miniature: redescription felt like explanation, and the felt explanation licensed extension to untestable domains.
3. The re-description is not neutral. **Persistence-as-optimisation has no term for wrong.** Describing a dominated party as free-energy-minimising is descriptively unobjectionable and normatively anaesthetic.
4. FEP requires a fixed state space; the programme's object is transformation of the state space. The framework's preconditions exclude its intended application.
5. "Critical computational sociology" occupies the contemplative stance Lukács identified as reification's subjective form, and has been attempted before as cybernetic systems theory, against which the Habermasian concept was formulated.

**Adjudication after all rounds: points 3, 4 and 5 were never refuted. Point 1 was reduced but not refuted — FEP's surviving contribution is a set of precise negative results (S-05, S-06), which is a real contribution and not the one sought. Point 2 was accepted as accurate about the source paper's specific arguments.**

# ROUND 1 — cross-examination

Selected exchanges. Every criticism targets a proposition.

**E3 → E1.** Strongest claim: FEP ≠ active inference ≠ predictive coding, and the source paper collapses them. Weakest: that precision `Γ` is socially applicable — precision-weighting presupposes an already-carved space of things to weight. Hidden assumption: that describing a system in terms of densities over states is ontologically innocent. **Survives:** the demand for typed specification, which is what kills F-01.

**E1 → E3.** Strongest claim: `Ω` presupposes individuation, therefore circularity. Weakest: the claim that formalisation "changes the subject" — this is unfalsifiable as stated. Hidden assumption: that the sedimented, already-individuated residue is not worth modelling because it is not the horizon. **E3's concession:** the residue is worth modelling and is where administrative power operates; it is simply not the Lebenswelt. **This concession is the substance of K-001's resolution.**

**E4 → E1.** `M_i → M_{i+1}` is not dialectical because model comparison requires a fixed `F`. **E1 agrees without reservation.** Recorded as a convergence from opposite motives — E1 defending mathematical precision, E4 defending Hegel from flattening. → S-02.

**E5 → E9.** `H1`–`H6` are all agent-internal; Habermas's concept is inter-agent and about coordination mechanism. **E9 accepts and withdraws the framing.** → F-08. E5 further notes that E9's demand for computational tractability is itself a candidate colonisation of the analysis — logged, not dismissed.

**E7 → E9.** `φ_{B→A}` as a total function is the imperial act as method. **E9's counter:** a formalism that *cannot* produce a reconciliation and whose output is an obstruction is not the same as one that denies loss. **E7's reply:** it is still an obstruction computed in your scheme. **Not resolved.** → K-009, and the invariance test Q-03 was constructed to decide it.

**E8 → all.** The Luhmann precedent applies to every agent's proposals equally. Any FEP-based social model must state what prevents its collapse into functionalist systems theory. **Only two answers survived:** locate the critical function outside the model (S-09), and measure coordination mechanisms rather than system functions (F-08).

**E6 → E5.** Is `R` vs. `S` codable by independent coders? **Unanswered — this is the empirical hinge on which `H8` stands or falls, and it is testable cheaply.** → Q-04.

**E10 → E1.** Name one social prediction FEP makes that network analysis plus institutional theory plus Hacking does not. **E1 declined to name one.** Recorded.

**Formal conflicts registered:**
`C(Lebenswelt = generative model) ∧ C(lifeworld is anonymous and pre-individuated) → ⊥`

# ROUND 4 — DESTROY / PRESERVE / ELEVATE

Outcomes recorded per proposition. Outcome A (reformulation) was reached three times; C (model-space transformation) twice; B (differentiation) three times; D (productive contradiction) twice; E (rejection) four times.

**P1 — `Lebenswelt = generative model`.**
DESTROY: unsupported leap (no typed specification), level confusion (individual index on a pre-individuated concept), hidden metaphysics (representationalism), historical distortion (no shared ancestry). PRESERVE: there is a shared background enabling communication, differentially distributed, and damageable. ELEVATE: **outcome E** at the level of identity; the residue is handled by F-02 under a different name. → D-01.

**P2 — `prediction error = contradiction`, `FE minimisation = sublation`.**
DESTROY: metric vs. structural; removal vs. determinate production; fixed vs. transformed criterion. PRESERVE: frameworks sometimes change through their own failures rather than external shock. ELEVATE: **outcome E** for the identity, **outcome B** for the residue (F-04 separates criterion-change from model-change). → D-02, S-02.

**P3 — institutions as super-agents with Markov blankets.**
DESTROY: reification (Pearl→Friston slide), erased internal politics (coalition model), Hegelian misattribution (organicism). PRESERVE: collectivities act in ways irreducible to individual intention. ELEVATE: **outcome C** — replace "is it an agent?" with "which conditional independences hold, over which variables, for how long, enforced by whom?" → D-03, D-06, S-01.

**P4 — empire as a dynamic rather than an entity.**
DESTROY: "active inference" adds nothing to "asymmetric relation"; without a differentia, every asymmetric relation is imperial and the term is empty. PRESERVE: the relational move is correct and anti-reifying. ELEVATE: **outcome A** — S-07, with the differentia supplied by clause (iii): the residue is rendered *nonexistent*, not merely unrecorded.

**P5 — colonization of the lifeworld as `H1`–`H7`.**
DESTROY: all of `H1`–`H6` are agent-internal and miss the concept; `H7` names a different mechanism. PRESERVE: several real and separable mechanisms were bundled. ELEVATE: **outcome B** — `H8` for colonization proper, `H5` retained as legibility, `H2` as attention capture, `H4` as foreclosure. → F-08, D-07, S-03.

**P6 — critical computational sociology.**
DESTROY: Lukács (the modelling stance *is* the contemplative stance); Luhmann (already attempted, and the critical concept was formulated against it); red-team point 3 (normative anaesthesia). PRESERVE: administrative classification is real, consequential, and its failures are recorded. ELEVATE: **outcome C** — relocate the critical function to the contestation surface. → S-09, D-08.

**P7 — `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B`, and formalising it.**
DESTROY (of the *formalisation*, not the claim): computing an obstruction converts equivocation into a quantity in the analyst's scheme. PRESERVE: the phenomenon is real and Bayesian formalism cannot represent it at all. ELEVATE: **outcome D — productive contradiction, deliberately unresolved**, with a decidable test constructed to break the tie (Q-03). → K-009, F-06.

**P8 — FEP's applicability to historical transformation.**
DESTROY: the fixed-state-space precondition excludes the intended application. PRESERVE: FEP-style modelling is legitimate intra-epochally. ELEVATE: **outcome A** for the restricted claim (S-05) and **a new concept** for the residue (S-06 — the state space as Absolute Knowing in measure-theoretic disguise). This is the only place in the cycle where the collision produced a concept none of the input frameworks possessed.

# ROUND 8 — Geist / Lebenswelt distinction

Tested: `Lebenswelt ~ 𝓛_t` (horizon/field of meaningful possibility), `Geist ~ G: 𝓛_t → 𝓛_{t+1}` (historical transformation of horizons).

- **[HEGEL]** Rejects. Geist is not an operator *on* horizons; it is self-relating and is the process coming to know *itself* as what it is. An operator has no self-relation, no recollection, and no *für uns*. Reducing Geist to a transition function removes precisely what distinguishes development from change. **Verdict: profound category error.**
- **[PHENOMENOLOGY]** Rejects `𝓛_t` first: horizon is not a field with members, and the time index presupposes an objective time-series the lifeworld is supposed to ground. **Verdict: category error at the first term, before Geist is reached.**
- **[FRANKFURT]** Notes the scheme reinstates philosophy of history as a single-sequence transformation — the target of the Frankfurt School's central objection to Hegel. `G` with one index is monolithic history restored as notation. **Verdict: historically indefensible; reproduces the universalism the source paper wanted to escape.**
- **[SOCIOLOGY]** No unit, no observation, no falsification. **Verdict: not a claim.**
- **[FEP]** `𝓛_t → 𝓛_{t+1}` where `𝓛` is a possibility space is exactly S-05: either both are contained in a fixed `𝓛*` (no novelty) or FEP does not apply. **Verdict: ill-formed.**
- **[COMPLEXITY]** Partially salvageable in one narrow sense: sedimented *classification schemes* do change over datable intervals, and the change is measurable. But that is `Ω_{c,t} → Ω_{c,t+1}` for a specific community and scheme, which is a modest empirical object and must not be called Geist.

**Consolidated verdict: profound category error as a Hegel interpretation; historically indefensible as a philosophy of history; partially salvageable only as a claim about datable change in institutional classification schemes, at which point the name should be dropped.** The distinction the source paper wanted — that Geist is historical rather than universal — is correct and is available directly from Hegel's *Volksgeist*/*Zeitgeist* and from Steinbock's generative phenomenology. It does not need, and is not improved by, an operator.

# ROUND 9 — adversarial empirical test

For each survivor: what situation distinguishes it from ordinary sociological explanation?

- **F-05 residuality.** Units: cases in an administrative system. Measurement: residual-category load, torque between biography and record, documented-vs-operative carving gap. Data: intake records, coding manuals, revision histories. Scale: months–years. Alternative hypothesis: residual growth reflects caseload, not carving failure — distinguishable by whether growth concentrates in specific case types. **Passes.**
- **F-07 looping.** Units: classified individuals. Design: staggered rollout. Alternative: the intervention triggered by the classification, not the classification, causes divergence — distinguishable where classification occurs without intervention. **Passes. Strongest existing evidence base (Hacking, Desrosières).**
- **F-08 `R→S`.** Units: decision sites. Measurement: reason given? contestable in kind? obligation to respond? substantive vs. procedural answer rate. Data: appeals and complaint records, moderation logs, benefits determinations. Alternative: `R→S` tracks caseload pressure rather than media-steering — distinguishable by whether it appears in low-volume high-stakes sites. **Passes, conditional on Q-04 (inter-coder reliability).**
- **F-06 contextuality.** Units: cases classified under two regimes. Measurement: overlap consistency; existence of a global section. **Conditionally passes** — has a genuine falsification condition (if overlap data always admits a global section, there is no object) but the analyst-frame invariance question Q-03 must be settled first.
- **F-04 `F_t` as variable.** Measurement: documented standards of adequacy. **Marginal.** Real data exist but the distinction from ordinary institutional-change history is not yet sharp.
- **Everything FEP-derived.** No surviving formalisation. Nothing to test.

# TERMINATION

Cycle 1 halted on **four simultaneous conditions**, not on exhaustion:

- **CONVERGENCE** — Pearl/Friston blankets and Lukácsian reification reached the same structure from unrelated traditions (S-01); E1 and E4 reached the fixed-functional result from opposite motives (S-02).
- **STABLE CONTRADICTION** — K-006 and K-009 remain coherent on both sides and are not currently reconcilable. They are Cycle 2's material.
- **CONCEPTUAL TRANSFORMATION** — "Where is the Markov blanket of empire?" was replaced by a relational predicate with a measurable differentia (S-07); "how do we model the lifeworld?" was replaced by "what does the declaration of a state space do, and who does it to whom?" (S-06).
- **FORMAL BOTTLENECK** — Q-01 is a genuine open problem; further argument will not produce the mathematics.

Continuing rounds on K-001–K-004 would be degenerative. Cycle 2 agenda is in `questions.md`.
