## K-001 — The transcendental circularity

**CLAIM A** `[MODEL-INFERENCE]` A formal model of the lifeworld can be constructed by specifying a state space `Ω`, a semantic structure `Σ`, and a salience structure `Γ`.

**CLAIM B** `[PHENOMENOLOGY]` Any such specification is itself an accomplishment carried out *within* a lifeworld, using its typifications, its sense of what counts as a state, and its criteria of relevance. The formalisation therefore presupposes what it claims to explain.

**TYPE** epistemological, ontological, representational

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** Not because B says modelling is impossible, but because B denies the model can be *about* its own condition of possibility. A treats the lifeworld as an object in the world; B treats it as the horizon within which objects appear. An object-level formalism cannot have its own horizon as an object without that horizon becoming an object *for a further horizon*, unformalised.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Yes, after distinction — but the distinction costs A most of what it wanted.** A may model the *sedimented residue*: those typifications that have already congealed into explicit, institutionally recorded categories. It may not model the horizonal function. The residue is where power operates administratively, so this is not a small domain; it is simply not the Lebenswelt.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Split `Lebenswelt` into `Lebenswelt_horizonal` (unformalisable in principle, not merely in practice) and `Sedimentation` (formalisable, institutionally recorded, historically indexed). Never let the second inherit the first's name. See `claims.md` C-02, C-03.

## K-002 — `Ω` presupposes individuation

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` `Ω_i` = space of intelligible states/categories, a component of the lifeworld.

**CLAIM B** `[PHENOMENOLOGY]` Horizon has *und-so-weiter* structure: determinable indeterminacy. It has no members. A set has members. The move from horizon to set is not a simplification but a change of category.

**TYPE** mathematical, ontological, representational

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** To write `Ω` one must already have carved the world into discriminable states. Horizon is the pre-predicative typicality that makes carving possible. `Ω` is an *output* of horizonal constitution being presented as its *definition*.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **No, as stated.** `Ω` cannot be *the* horizon. It can be an *image* of a horizon under a specific institutional carving — which immediately makes the carving, not the space, the theoretically loaded object. This inverts the research programme productively: **the interesting variable is the carving map, not the space.**

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Replace `Ω_i` as a primitive with a *carving* `κ: (practice) → (measurable space)`, and study `κ` — who performs it, under what authority, with what residue. See `formalizations.md` F-05.

## K-003 — Graded error vs. ungraded contradiction

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` prediction error ≈ contradiction; free-energy minimisation ≈ sublation.

**CLAIM B** `[HEGEL]` Contradiction is a structural self-undermining of a determination, without magnitude. Prediction error is a precision-weighted residual with a norm.

**TYPE** mathematical, ontological, terminological

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** One admits "small," the other does not. A metric quantity and a structural relation cannot be identified. Further: minimisation *removes* discrepancy while leaving the framework intact; determinate negation *destroys the framework* and yields new content from that destruction.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **No.** The identification is rejected. What survives is much narrower: *some* episodes of scientific and institutional change involve an agent's evaluative standard being revised by the very failures it was used to diagnose, and FEP has no formalism for that. The residual insight belongs to Hegel, not to FEP.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Make the evaluative functional a variable: study `F_t` alongside `M_t`. See `survivors.md` S-02, `formalizations.md` F-04.

## K-004 — Scale confusion inside the `(Ω, Π, Σ, Γ)` bundle

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` The four components are aspects of one object `L_i`.

**CLAIM B** `[SOCIOLOGY]`+`[COMPLEXITY]` They have different bearers, different timescales, and different observability. `Γ` (salience) shifts in weeks and is visible in attention and audit data. `Π` (practical availability) shifts with law, budget and technology over months to years. `Σ` (semantic relation) shifts over decades. `Ω` (intelligibility) shifts over generations and is typically visible only in its breakdowns.

**TYPE** scalar, methodological, empirical

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** Bundling licenses inference across four orders of temporal magnitude. A measured shift in `Γ` would be reported as a shift in "the lifeworld," which is an ecological fallacy with extra steps.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Yes, at different descriptive levels — but only if the bundle is dissolved.** Keep four separate objects with four separate measurement strategies and four separate falsification conditions. Prohibit inference from one to another without an explicit coupling hypothesis.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Dissolve `L_i`. Retain `Γ_{c,t}`, `Π_{c,t}`, `Σ_{c,t}`, `Ω_{c,t}` as independently measured, explicitly coupled objects.

## K-005 — The state space is a hidden Absolute

**CLAIM A** `[FEP]` FEP requires a system at NESS with a fixed state space carrying a stationary density.

**CLAIM B** `[HEGEL]`+`[ANTHROPOLOGY-STS]`+`[FRANKFURT]` The phenomenon of interest is historical transformation of what is intelligible at all.

**TYPE** mathematical, ontological, historical, normative

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** If `Ω → Ω'` is real transformation, no fixed measure space contains both, and FEP does not apply. If both live in a pre-given `Ω*`, then every future form of life is already a point in a space specified now — which is Absolute Knowing asserted as a measure-theoretic convenience, and which makes historical novelty definitionally impossible.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **No. This is a genuine dilemma, and it is the most important result of Cycle 1.** Both horns are damaging: horn (i) is unfalsifiable and covertly metaphysical; horn (ii) concedes that FEP cannot address the programme's central phenomenon.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Either (α) restrict FEP-style modelling explicitly to *intra-epochal* dynamics, with epoch boundaries identified by non-FEP means, and never claim to model the boundary crossing; or (β) adopt a formalism whose objects do not require a total pre-given space — presheaves over a growing site, or a category whose objects are added as they are constituted. Route (β) is the research frontier. See `questions.md` Q-01, Q-02.

## K-006 — The modeller's stance is the reified stance

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` A critical computational sociology can perform immanent critique using the tools of the system it critiques.

**CLAIM B** `[FRANKFURT — Lukács]` The contemplative stance of the calculating observer who predicts law-like regularity but cannot intervene *qua* observer is the subjective form of reification itself. The modeller does not risk reification; the modeller occupies its paradigm case.

**TYPE** epistemological, normative, methodological

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A's remedy (better modelling, more reflexivity) is drawn from within the practice B identifies as the disease. Reflexivity performed by the observer, about the observer, remains observation.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **[CYCLE 1: unresolved, exit proposed via S-09.] [CYCLE 2: S-09 DESTROYED; K-006 PARTIALLY RESOLVED WITH A PERMANENT RESIDUE — outcome B + C.]**

**CYCLE 2 DESTRUCTION OF S-09** (`cycle2_rounds.md` Part II). Instrumenting the contestation surface renders *mētis* legible — the operation identified as violent in the same document that proposed it. It has a documented failure mode with a name: **legal endogeneity** (Edelman, *Working Law*) — grievance and compliance structures that courts defer to as evidence of compliance while producing no substantive change. A measured contestation surface becomes a compliance metric and "healthy appeal rates" becomes a target. **S-09 v1 was a recipe for endogeneity.** → `dead_ends.md` D-10.

**CYCLE 2 RESOLUTION.** The conflated term was "contestation." Split it:
- **token-contestation** — objecting to the *application* of a category to a case; operates inside the carving; this is F-08's `R`.
- **type-contestation** — objecting to the *category*, with power to force revision of `κ`; operates on the carving.

Every existing recourse mechanism in algorithmic governance is token-level. **Level-2 colonization** = no forum exists in which category-revision is a possible speech act with institutional uptake. → `formalizations.md` F-10.

The Lukácsian exit is therefore **not better data and not more reflexivity** — the contemplative stance is the separation of knowing from doing before regularity taken as given. The criterion is **who holds revisionary authority over `κ`**, which is *constitutional, not methodological*, and cannot be satisfied by any modelling practice because it is not about modelling.

**PERMANENT RESIDUE:** an analyst describing the distribution of revisionary authority is still an analyst, and the description is available to those holding the authority, for legitimation. **There is no formalism-side escape from Lukács.** What Cycle 2 delivers is a criterion for when a practice is *less* reified rather than more. Residue migrated to **K-011**.

**CONVERGENCE NOTE:** this result and `survivors.md` S-06 were reached independently — one from Lukács via Edelman, one from FEP's measure-theoretic preconditions. The unrevisable `Ω*` and the absence of type-contestation are **one condition described in two vocabularies.**

## K-011 — Explicitness is not authority `[OPEN — the single stable contradiction of Cycle 2]`

**CLAIM A** `[MODEL-INFERENCE]` Making the frame declaration explicit — the cover, the carving, the partition, the state space — is a genuine critical gain, because a declared commitment can be contested while a silent one cannot.

**CLAIM B** `[ANTHROPOLOGY-STS]`+`[FRANKFURT]` An explicit declaration made *by the analyst* is still the analyst's. Explicitness relocates the imperial act into the open without transferring any authority over it. A described party who can now see the frame that binds them, and still cannot revise it, has gained legibility of their own subjection.

**TYPE** epistemological, normative, methodological, representational

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A treats visibility as the precondition of contestation. B observes that visibility has repeatedly functioned as a *substitute* for contestation — the same structure as legal endogeneity, one level up. The disclosure becomes the remedy.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Unresolved, and now understood to be the *only* stable contradiction remaining.** The three separate residues logged in Cycle 2 — E7's dissent on the cover (I.4), the analyst residue on authority (II.4), and the process's own self-implication (III.4) — are the same contradiction appearing at three scales. Further argument will restate it.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** None available by argument. What would settle it is empirical: **do institutions where the frame was made explicit show more type-level revision than institutions where it was not?** If explicitness without transferred authority produces no revisions, B wins decisively and the entire "transparency" family of remedies is exposed as level-2 endogeneity. See `questions.md` Q-11.

## K-007 — Habermas's system/lifeworld boundary is not neutral

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` Colonization of the lifeworld can be modelled by measuring the encroachment of system logic into lifeworld domains.

**CLAIM B** `[FRANKFURT — Fraser 1985; Honneth]` The system/lifeworld boundary is itself an ideological product: classing the family as lifeworld conceals that it is power-structured, and the distinction naturalises the gendered division of labour.

**TYPE** normative, empirical, methodological

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A must fix the boundary to measure crossings of it. B says the placement of the boundary is the contested political fact. Operationalisation freezes the contest and reports the winning side as nature.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Yes, after distinction, at real cost.** Do not operationalise "system domain" and "lifeworld domain" as fixed regions. Operationalise the **coordination mechanism** locally, per decision-site — which is measurable without pre-classifying the domain — and treat the *domain* question as an output of the analysis rather than an input.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Measure mechanism, not territory. See `formalizations.md` F-08.

## K-008 — FEP universalism vs. anthropological non-universality

**CLAIM A** `[FEP]` Any persisting thing with a blanket can be described as minimising free energy; this holds for cells, brains, and in principle collectives.

**CLAIM B** `[ANTHROPOLOGY-STS]` A framework applying equally to everything supplies no discriminating description of anything specific, and its apparent neutrality conceals the culturally particular carving of the state space required to apply it.

**TYPE** epistemological, methodological

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A's generality is claimed as strength. B says generality at that level is purchased by pushing all the empirical content into the unexamined choice of variables — where it becomes invisible and unaccountable.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Yes, at different descriptive levels, with a strict rule.** FEP may function as an *organising grammar* for building specific models. It may never be cited as *evidence* for a social claim. Any social claim must be carried by the specific model's specific commitments, which must be stated and are falsifiable.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Prohibit "FEP predicts…" in social contexts. Require "this generative model, with this `Ω`, chosen by us for these reasons, predicts…".

## K-009 — Does formalising incommensurability destroy it?

**CLAIM A** `[MODEL-INFERENCE]` Sheaf-theoretic contextuality formalises `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B` honestly: local sections agree pairwise, no global section exists, and the obstruction is measurable.

**CLAIM B** `[ANTHROPOLOGY-STS — Viveiros de Castro; Strathern]` Controlled equivocation is a *practice* of keeping equivocation visible. A formalism that *computes* the obstruction has converted the equivocation into a quantity in the anthropologist's own `Ω` — the imperial move, performed more elegantly.

**TYPE** representational, normative, methodological

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A claims to represent the failure of representation. B holds that a represented failure of representation is a representation, and the residue that mattered is once again the residue.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **[CYCLE 1: unresolved.] [CYCLE 2: RESOLVED BY RELOCATING THE CONTESTED OBJECT — outcome B + A.]**

**CYCLE 2 RESOLUTION** (`cycle2_rounds.md` Part I). The invariance test was run. **B is correct on the technical point:** the obstruction is cover-relative, and both degenerate limits — coarsening to one context, refining to singletons — drive it to zero by construction. **A survives anyway, differently:** the cover is not a mathematical convenience but the substantive empirical claim about which questions can be asked *jointly in a single practical setting*, which is ethnographically answerable per site. F-06 therefore does not measure incommensurability; **it converts the assertion of incommensurability into a defensible, contestable empirical claim, and forces the analyst to declare it** — where a common-`Ω` Bayesian model assumes the same thing silently.

Two consequences neither side anticipated:
1. **Contextuality is a middle-range phenomenon.** Total opacity has no overlaps and is non-contextual; total assimilation has one context and is non-contextual. **Incommensurability is not alterity.** The formalism models the incommensurability *internal to sustained contact* — which is exactly where controlled equivocation arises.
2. **Empire does not resolve the contradiction; it coarsens the cover**, so the obstruction becomes unmeasurable while the loss relocates into residual-category load. F-06 and F-05 are anti-correlated detectors, and the transition between regimes is the observable event. → `formalizations.md` F-09.

**STANDING DISSENT (E7, not withdrawn):** making the cover explicit is still the *analyst's* explicit declaration, and nothing in the formalism gives the described party authority over it. Migrated to **K-011**.

## K-010 — Is the Karp lineage evidence or coincidence?

**CLAIM A** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]` A Frankfurt-trained sociologist built the paradigm instrument of lifeworld colonization; the theory was operationalised, possibly deliberately.

**CLAIM B** `[SOCIOLOGY]`+`[HISTORY-OF-SCIENCE]` Biographical lineage is not causal evidence about a technology's function. Data-integration platforms built by people with no critical-theory training have the same effects. The lineage is rhetorically powerful and evidentially near-empty.

**TYPE** empirical, historical, normative

**WHY THEY CONFLICT** A treats the biography as explanatory; B as ironic decoration.

**CAN BOTH SURVIVE?** **Yes, after distinction.** The lineage is legitimate as a *case study in the reversibility of critique* — a documented instance of critical vocabulary being available to, and not preventing, the construction of the thing criticised. That is a real finding about critique, not about Palantir. As a causal claim about the technology it must be dropped.

**REQUIRED TRANSFORMATION** Reclassify from evidence-about-technology to evidence-about-critique. The generalisable question — *what makes a critical theory operationalisable by its target?* — is more valuable than the biographical one. See `questions.md` Q-06.
