# dead_ends.md

Nothing here is deleted. Genealogy is retained so that a later cycle can see what was tried and why it failed, and so that failed ideas are not silently re-imported under new names.

---

## D-01 — `Lebenswelt = the human-level generative model`

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper, "Habermas and the Lebenswelt."

**Why it failed:** over-determined refutation from four disciplines with no shared premises (`survivors.md` S-04). The decisive detail is the smallest one: **the index `i`.** Husserl's lifeworld is anonymous and pre-individuated; Habermas's is a set of reproduction processes carried by a communication community. Neither is a per-person object. A per-person object is a *worldview*, which is a perfectly good sociological concept and is not the lifeworld.

**What it was reaching for:** that there is a shared background making communication possible, that it can be differentially distributed, and that it can be damaged. All three are true and none requires the identification.

**Do not re-import as:** "the collective generative model," "shared priors," "cultural priors." These are the same claim with the subscript hidden.

## D-02 — `prediction error = contradiction`, `free-energy minimisation = sublation`

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper, "Hegel and Friston, naturalizing the dialectic."

**Why it failed:** three independent structural mismatches. (i) Prediction error is metric and graded; contradiction is structural and ungraded — there is no small contradiction. (ii) Minimisation *removes* discrepancy, leaving the framework intact; determinate negation *destroys* the framework and generates determinate content from that destruction. (iii) Decisive: model comparison needs a fixed evidence functional, Aufhebung transforms the functional (`survivors.md` S-02).

**What it was reaching for:** that systems sometimes change in ways that are not adjustments within a framework but changes of framework, and that such changes are driven by the framework's own failures rather than by external shock. Correct, important, and Hegelian; unavailable in FEP.

**Note on the ELEVATE outcome:** this is a rejection (outcome E) at the level of identity and a differentiation (outcome B) at the level of what remains. It is not outcome A. The protocol's warning against forcing reformulation was load-bearing here.

## D-03 — Institutions / Geist as super-agents with Markov blankets

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper, "When is the agent an empire?"

**Why it failed:** three routes. (i) Hegel: Geist is a normative achievement sustained by mutual recognition, not a larger organism; Hegel explicitly rejected Romantic organicism about the state, so this reads Hegel as the position he attacked. (ii) Sociology: firms are coalitions with conflicting goals (Cyert & March); Arrow forbids non-dictatorial aggregation; assigning one policy `π` to an institution erases the internal politics that is the object of study. (iii) Complexity: collective agenthood is a *testable* claim about informational closure of a specified macro-variable, and for most institutions it holds only in narrow channels (financial reporting, formal decision procedures, legal personhood), not globally.

**What it was reaching for:** that collectivities sometimes act in ways not reducible to any member's intention. True. Handled by governance, procedure and network structure — not by attributing a mind.

**Superseded by:** `survivors.md` S-07 (imperial as a relational predicate) and the closure test in `definitions.md`.

## D-04 — Hegel §339 as textual support for Markov blankets

**Provenance:** the cited FEP–Hegel paper, endorsed in the source paper.

**Why it failed:** the passage concerns *Bestimmtheit* in the logic of the Concept. "Bounded things are bounded" is entailed by every theory of boundaries ever proposed and therefore discriminates between none of them. Zero information. This is the textbook case of retrospective conceptual similarity presented as conceptual ancestry.

**Unresolved bibliographic issue:** the cited paper, "The Dialectics of Free Energy Minimization," is referenced in the source paper without author or venue. Its argument should be read directly before further use. See `questions.md` Q-09.

## D-05 — The afterimage as evidence for predictive processing

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper, "On agent-based modeling."

**Why it failed:** negative afterimages are standardly explained by photoreceptor bleaching and opponent-channel adaptation — peripheral, retinal, non-inferential. A phenomenon compatible with a framework is not evidence for it, and this one has a well-supported non-inferential explanation.

**Why it is recorded rather than passed over:** it is a clean instance of the red team's central charge. The framework's vocabulary is general enough to redescribe the phenomenon, redescription feels like explanation, and the felt explanatory power then licenses extension to domains where no test is available at all. The mechanism by which the source paper came to trust FEP for society is visible in miniature here.

## D-06 — "Where is the Markov blanket of empire?"

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper.

**Why it failed as posed:** it requests a Friston blanket — a real boundary in the world — from a formalism that supplies only Pearl blankets, constructs internal to a chosen model (`survivors.md` S-01). The question presupposes what would have to be demonstrated.

**Replaced by (outcome C, model-space transformation):** *which conditional independences actually hold, over which variables, for how long, and who enforces them?* Answerable, and the answers are sociological rather than ontological.

## D-07 — `H1`–`H6` as candidate formalisations of colonization of the lifeworld

**Provenance:** research-problem specification.

**Why they failed:** all six are transformations internal to one agent. Habermas's thesis is about the mechanism coordinating action *between* agents. `H7` is inter-agent but names Scott's legibility, a genuinely different mechanism with a different remedy.

**Retained separately, not as colonization:** `H5` (`Ω → Ω'`) survives as *ontological imposition* / legibility; `H2` (`Γ → Γ'`) survives as *attention capture*, which is a real and separately measurable pathology; `H4` (`Π → Π'`) survives as *foreclosure of practical possibility*. Each is a distinct mechanism. Calling them all "colonization" was the conflation the round was designed to detect.

**Superseded by:** `formalizations.md` F-08 (`H8`).

## D-08 — "Critical computational sociology" as a self-sufficient programme

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper's central proposal.

**Why it failed in its self-sufficient form:** Lukács's objection is not that modelling risks reification but that the calculating-observer stance *is* reification's subjective form (`contradictions.md` K-006). A model cannot be its own critique. Additionally, the programme has a historical precedent — cybernetic systems theory — against which the critical concept it wants to use was itself formulated (`survivors.md` S-08).

**Survives in modified form:** as an asymmetric division of labour — computational instrumentation of the *contestation surface* (appeals, refusals, residual categories, torque), with the critical function located in the classified population's record of resistance rather than in the model (`survivors.md` S-09). This is a smaller and more defensible programme than the one proposed, and it is implementable now.

## D-09 — Karp's biography as evidence about Palantir's technical function

**Provenance:** `[USER-HYPOTHESIS]`, source paper, "On Alex Karp."

**Why it failed as evidence:** lineage is not causation about a technology's effects. Comparable platforms built by people with no critical-theory training produce the same effects, which means the biography does no explanatory work on the technical claim.

**Reclassified, not discarded:** it is a documented case of *critical vocabulary being available to, and not preventing, the construction of the thing criticised*. That is evidence about critique — and the generalisable question it raises (`questions.md` Q-06) is more valuable than the biographical one. Two premises also require primary-source verification before load-bearing use (`claims.md` C-16).

## D-10 — S-09 v1: "instrument the contestation surface"

**Provenance:** `[CROSS-DISCIPLINARY-SYNTHESIS]`, Cycle 1's proposed exit from K-006. Killed in Cycle 2.

**Why it failed:** two specific attacks, not one general worry.
1. **It renders *mētis* legible.** Collecting appeals, refusals and workarounds as data performs on resistance exactly the operation the same document identified as violent when a state performs it on peasant agriculture. The proposal was Scott's state, applied to dissent.
2. **It has a documented failure mode with a name — legal endogeneity** (Edelman, *Working Law*). Organisations build grievance and compliance structures; courts defer to their existence as evidence of compliance; substantive change does not follow. A measured contestation surface becomes a compliance metric, and "healthy appeal rates" becomes a target to be managed rather than a signal to be heeded.

**What it was reaching for:** that the classified are not in the contemplative position. **True, and it survives untouched.** The error was in the verb — it converted a claim about *position* into a proposal for a *dataset*, which is the contemplative stance recapturing its own critique. That the error took this specific form is worth recording: it is the most seductive available mistake for anyone in this research programme.

**Superseded by:** `survivors.md` S-13 and `formalizations.md` F-10 — the token/type contestation distinction, and revisionary authority over `κ` as a **constitutional** rather than methodological criterion. Note that the successor is explicitly *not* something a modeller can do, which is why it survives where S-09 v1 did not.

**Do not re-import as:** "participatory data collection," "contestability metrics," "algorithmic recourse rates," "community feedback loops." All four are `T₁` instruments presented as `T₂` remedies.
