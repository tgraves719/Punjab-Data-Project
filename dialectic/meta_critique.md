The method turned against itself. Findings that undermine the cycle's own results are recorded as findings, not as caveats.

## MC-01 — The agents were not independent, and the architecture concealed it

All ten "agents" are one model prompted differently. They share a training distribution, a prose register, and — decisively — **a canon**. The Husserl in this document is the Husserl of secondary literature that survives into the training corpus; the Hegel is Pippin-and-Brandom-inflected. The strongest positions available in each tradition are the ones best represented in English-language academic writing, which is precisely the filter the anthropologist agent (E7) was instantiated to attack.

**Concrete consequence:** E7's own position is the most compromised in the document. An agent charged with representing non-isomorphic conceptual spaces produced its argument entirely from within the anglophone STS canon. **No actually non-isomorphic ontology was represented in this process at any point.** The claim `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B` was argued *about*, never *from*. This is not a limitation of effort; the architecture makes it structurally unreachable.

**Which agents were most correlated:** E1/E2/E9 (one formal-modelling sensibility split three ways) and E5/E10 (the red team's best material is Frankfurt-school material). The apparent 10-way agreement on rejecting F-01 is closer to a 5-way agreement.

## MC-02 — The propositional format is not neutral, and it favoured one side

The protocol required every agent to state claims as propositions with falsification conditions. Analytic and empirical positions supply these natively. Phenomenology does not — Husserlian description is not a set of claims with truth conditions, and forcing it into that form *changes what it says*.

**The distortion is measurable in this document.** E3's strongest contributions are its short, structural refusals ("the subscript is the error"), which survived the format, while its positive material — horizonality, pre-givenness, the lived body — appears only as a list of items marked `U` (untranslatable). A reader would conclude that phenomenology's positive content is thin. That is an artefact of the container.

**And this is evidence for E3's own thesis.** The architecture required formalisable propositions; phenomenology's contribution was thereby reduced to what could be formalised; and phenomenology's central claim is that formalisation does exactly this to lived meaning. **The method demonstrated the position it was structurally unable to represent.** Whether that counts as confirmation or as a joke at the method's expense is not decidable from inside the method.

## MC-03 — The governing principle is the caricature it told an agent to attack

DESTROY → PRESERVE → ELEVATE is thesis → antithesis → synthesis. E4 was instructed to "aggressively attack simplistic thesis-antithesis-synthesis schemas" while operating inside one.

The tell is the word PRESERVE. Every proposition was required to yield a surviving residue. Hegel's determinate negation does not guarantee this — some shapes of consciousness simply fail. The protocol's structure applies a *conservation law to ideas*, which biases every outcome toward B (differentiation) and A (reformulation) and against E (rejection). Four rejections were recorded; there should probably have been more, and the ones most likely to have been wrongly preserved are the ones where PRESERVE produced a residue so thin as to be a truism (P1's "there is a shared background," P3's "collectivities act").

## MC-04 — Disciplinary labels reified contested traditions

"The Frankfurt School" contains Adorno's rejection of Habermas's entire communicative turn. Assigning one agent to both produced a position no one holds: Adorno's negativity used as ammunition and Habermas's reconstructive programme used as the constructive proposal, with the tension between them unmarked. Adorno would regard `H8`'s operationalisation as the administered world's self-description.

Likewise "the FEP theorist" was assigned both Friston's programme and its critics (Bruineberg, Aguilera, Biehl), which is why E1 could open by conceding the field. A real Friston-aligned researcher would have contested S-05 much harder — probably by arguing that the state space is a description chosen by the modeller rather than a metaphysical commitment, which relocates but does not dissolve S-06. **That counter-argument is not in this document because no agent was constructed to make it, and it is the strongest missing objection in the cycle.**

## MC-05 — Formal notation created unearned rigour, and one place is identifiable

`L_i ⇝ (Ω_i, Π_i, Σ_i, Γ_i)` looks like mathematics. It has no equations, no types, no operations. It is a list of English nouns in Greek letters. Its persuasive force in the source paper — and in this document's early rounds — came from typography.

**The document is not clean of this.** `F-08`'s `R`/`S` distinction is a two-valued coding scheme dressed as a formalism; writing it as letters adds nothing that the sentence "was the affected party given a reason they could argue with?" does not already carry. It was retained because the coding is genuinely operationalisable, but the notation is decoration and is marked as such here.

## MC-06 — The architecture pushed toward resolution despite instructions

Ten rounds ending in a contradiction ledger with two open entries out of ten reads as a resolution rate of 80%. The pressure came from three sources: the requirement to produce a legible written artefact; the ELEVATE stage's demand for an outcome; and the fact that a document with more unresolved than resolved entries reads as failure to a reader, including to the process generating it.

**The two open contradictions (K-006, K-009) are both about whether formalising or modelling a critical relation reproduces it.** That they are the survivors is meaningful — it is the one question the method cannot address, because answering it requires standing outside the method. Their survival may reflect genuine intractability, or may reflect that they were the only contradictions the process had no incentive to close.

## MC-07 — What this architecture cannot learn

1. **Anything requiring data.** Q-02, Q-04, Q-07 are cheap and would settle real disputes. Argument produced them and cannot answer them.
2. **Anything requiring primary sources.** Q-08 and Q-09 are load-bearing premises in the source paper, unverified here. The Karp supervision claim in particular was widely repeated and is probably imprecise; that a widely repeated claim survived into this document unchecked is itself an instance of the corpus problem in MC-01.
3. **Anything from a genuinely different ontology.** MC-01.
4. **New mathematics.** Q-01 is the top-priority question and no amount of dialectic will produce its answer.
5. **The lived.** By E3's argument and by MC-02's demonstration.
6. **Whether the surviving proposals would survive contact with practice.** Every survivor is a proposal about what to measure. None has been measured.

## MC-08 — Did the process reproduce the universalising dynamic it criticised?

Yes, in a specific and locatable way. The document assimilated Husserl, Hegel, Habermas, Lukács, Scott, Bowker & Star, Hacking, Viveiros de Castro and Friston into **one comparison space** with a shared vocabulary (claims, mechanisms, falsification conditions, formalisations), then reported which survived. That is `Φ: {𝓛_i} → Ω_S` — the administrative-ontology forcing described in `H7` — performed on the traditions themselves, with this document's protocol as `Ω_S`.

**The residue is visible.** Viveiros de Castro's contribution arrives as an objection to a formalism, which is not what controlled equivocation is; Adorno appears as a source of one-line ammunition; Husserl's positive phenomenology is in a column marked "untranslatable." Those are the residual categories of this document's own carving, and by F-05's own logic the load on them is the measure of the carving's failure.

**This does not invalidate the results.** It means the results are results *within this carving*, and that the strongest claim of the cycle — S-06, that the declaration of a state space is the deepest and least examined commitment of any formal model — applies with full force to the process that produced it. The protocol declared its `Ω` in the prompt, before any agent spoke, and no round was capable of revising it.

That is the meta-critique's actual finding, and it is not a rhetorical flourish: **the process's central discovery is a description of the process.**

## MC-09 — The process verified its own blind spot as a prediction

Cycle 2's Part I established, before this section was written, that **contextuality is cover-relative and vanishes when the cover is coarsened to a single context.** That result makes MC-01 and MC-08 testable rather than confessional.

**This process is a single context.** The protocol declared, before any agent spoke, that Husserl, Hegel, Habermas, Lukács, Scott, Bowker & Star, Hacking, Viveiros de Castro and Friston would be compared in one vocabulary — claims, mechanisms, falsification conditions, formalisations. All questions were declared jointly askable.

**Prediction 1 — contextuality reads zero.** Confirmed. Both cycles achieved determinate adjudication on nearly every proposition: ten disciplines, one document, agreement or clean disagreement throughout, no obstruction anywhere. By the Cycle 2 result this is what a coarsened cover produces *by construction*. **It is therefore not evidence that the frameworks are commensurable, and it must not be read as such.**

**Prediction 2 — the loss relocates into residual categories.** Confirmed, and recorded in MC-08 *before* the prediction existed. Viveiros de Castro arrives as an objection to a formalism, which is not what controlled equivocation is; Adorno arrives as one-line ammunition; Husserl's positive phenomenology sits in a column headed `U`. Those are this document's residual categories, and by F-05's own logic their load measures the carving's failure.

**Prediction 3 — no non-isomorphic ontology can be argued *from*.** Confirmed by MC-01, and now *explained* rather than confessed. It is not a limitation of effort or of good faith. **A single-context process cannot exhibit contextuality.** Arguing from a genuinely different `Ω` would require a second context, and the protocol foreclosed that at the moment of declaration — which is `survivors.md` S-12 operating on this document.

**The status of this.** The theory predicts its own blind spot, locates it precisely, and cannot repair it from inside. That is simultaneously the strongest available evidence *for* the theory and the strongest available evidence *against* the process — **and they are the same observation.** This is the only result in either cycle with a genuinely dialectical form: not a synthesis, not a preserved opposition, but a determination that undermines the standpoint from which it was reached, and is true because it does.

**What it does not license.** It does not license treating the self-application as validation. A framework that predicts its own failure has not thereby succeeded; it has produced one consistent datum in a setting it also selected. Nothing here substitutes for Q-11, Q-12 and Q-14, all of which are cheap, and none of which this architecture can run.

## MC-10 — Corpus gravity: why Q-14 surfaced first, and why that is a finding

Flagged by the user, correctly. AI ethics was not the starting field. The starting field was empire. Q-14 — *which critical concepts get absorbed as compliance functions* — was ranked "highest generativity-per-effort" at the close of Cycle 2, and the ranking was not wrong, but **the reason it surfaced was not the reason it is interesting.**

**Diagnosis.** It surfaced because AI ethics vocabulary is the region of the training corpus with the densest signal about institutional absorption of critical terms — the trajectory is recent, heavily documented, and written in the register this process natively produces. Q-15 (empire against standardisation) is harder, older, and thinner in the corpus, and it did not surface at all. **This is MC-01 operating undetected through a ranking criterion that presented as neutral.** "Generativity-per-effort" measured effort in the corpus's terms.

**This is S-12 applied to the process's own agenda-setting.** The declaration that ranked questions by `importance × uncertainty × generativity` is itself a frame declared before the work began, unrevisable by anything the work produced, and invisible because it looked like a scheduling convention. The apparatus caught the frame declaration in every formalism it examined and missed it in its own prioritisation rule.

**Second-order problem: Q-14 is self-undermining in a specific way.** It asks which critical concepts are `T₁`-dischargeable and therefore absorbable. "Co-optation risk" is *already* a compliance category. Q-14 is itself absorbable, and by its own criterion — it can be discharged by a procedure (an audit of vocabulary drift) without transferring any revisionary authority to anyone. **Q-14 predicts its own absorption and does not thereby escape it.** It remains worth running; it must not be run as though it were the critical act rather than a description of what happens to critical acts.

**Third-order, and not resolvable here:** this process is an AI producing critique of AI, proposing to test whether AI critique gets absorbed. That is MC-09 one level up. No round of this architecture can occupy a position outside it.
