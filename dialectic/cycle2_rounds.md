# PART I — Q-03: is the contextuality obstruction invariant under change of the analyst's frame?

This was flagged as decidable mathematics that should be settled before more philosophy. It is, and settling it transforms K-009.

## I.1 The answer is no, and the way it fails is the finding

Contextuality in the Abramsky–Brandenburger setting is defined **relative to a measurement cover** `𝓜` — the family of contexts, each context being a set of questions that can be asked *jointly*. An empirical model assigns a distribution to each context; the model is contextual when these agree on all overlaps yet no global distribution marginalises to all of them.

Change the cover and the obstruction changes. Two degenerate limits:

- **Coarsen to one context containing everything.** If all questions are declared jointly askable, the empirical model *is* a global section. Contextuality reads **zero**, by construction.
- **Refine to singletons.** With no overlap constraints beyond marginals, a product distribution is always a global section. Contextuality reads **zero**, again by construction.

So the obstruction is not frame-invariant, and E7's objection lands. But the *shape* of the failure was not anticipated by either side:

> **Contextuality is a phenomenon of intermediate covers only.** It requires contexts large enough to constrain and overlapping enough to conflict, and small enough that no single context contains everything.

## I.2 What this means for incommensurability

Translated back:

- **Total mutual opacity** — two communities with no shared practical settings — has no overlaps, hence no obstruction. **Formally non-contextual.**
- **Complete assimilation** — one administrative context in which all questions are jointly askable — has a global section by construction. **Formally non-contextual.**
- **Sustained partial contact** — translation succeeding locally, everywhere, and failing globally — is the only regime in which the obstruction exists.

This is a real distinction and neither input framework had it in this form:

> **Incommensurability is not alterity.** Radical otherness is not contextual; it is disconnected. The sheaf formalism cannot represent the wholly other, and this is a delimitation rather than a defect: what it models is *the incommensurability internal to sustained contact*, which is precisely the site of Viveiros de Castro's controlled equivocation — equivocation arises in translation, not in isolation.

## I.3 The consequence that reverses the round

If coarsening the cover drives the obstruction to zero, then:

> **Empire does not resolve incommensurability. It coarsens the cover, so that the obstruction becomes unmeasurable while the loss remains.**

Imposing a single administrative ontology *is* the declaration that all questions are jointly askable. Under that declaration the mathematics reports perfect consistency. Nothing is contradictory any more; there is one context, and everything in it agrees.

The loss does not vanish. It relocates — into residual-category load, into torque between biography and record, into the growth of "other." Which is `formalizations.md` F-05.

> **F-06 and F-05 are complementary detectors, anti-correlated across the imperial process.** Contextuality is measurable during contested contact and falls to zero on assimilation. Residuality is low during contested contact and rises on assimilation. **The transition between the two regimes is the observable event, and neither measure alone can see it.**

This is F-09 in `formalizations.md`. It is falsifiable — the prediction is a specific anti-correlation in paired classification data across periods of institutional consolidation — and it is not derivable from either Scott or Viveiros de Castro alone.

## I.4 K-009 adjudicated

**CLAIM A** (the modeler): contextuality formalises `Ω_A ≇ Ω_B` honestly.
**CLAIM B** (the anthropologist): computing an obstruction converts equivocation into a quantity in the analyst's scheme.

**B is correct on the technical point and A survives anyway, differently.**

The obstruction *is* cover-relative. But the cover is not a mathematical convenience — **the cover is the substantive empirical claim about which questions can be asked together in a single practical setting.** That is an ethnographic question with a determinate answer per site, and it is exactly the kind of question ethnography is for.

So F-06 does not measure incommensurability. **It converts the assertion of incommensurability into a defensible empirical claim about the structure of joint askability, and makes the analyst declare that claim in a form that can be contested.** Compare the Bayesian alternative, where a shared `Ω` is assumed *silently* and the assumption is never stated at all.

**Outcome: B (differentiation) plus A (reformulation).** K-009 moves from `unresolved` to `resolved by relocating the contested object` — from the obstruction to the cover. The anthropologist's objection is preserved intact and redirected: **do not attack the number, attack the cover, and the formalism has already put the cover where you can reach it.**

This is not a synthesis. E7 records a standing dissent: making the cover explicit is still making it *the analyst's* explicit declaration, and nothing here gives the described party authority over it. That dissent is answered — partially — in Part II, and only by leaving formalism entirely.

# PART II — K-006: the modeller occupies the contemplative stance

Cycle 1's exit was S-09: relocate the critical function to the contestation record. Cycle 2's first task is to destroy it.

## II.1 DESTROY S-09

The attack is specific, not general.

**Instrumenting the contestation surface is surveillance of resistance.** Collecting appeals, refusals, and workarounds as data renders *mētis* legible — which is the operation identified as violent in the same document that proposed it. S-09 proposed to do to resistance what Scott's state does to peasant agriculture.

**Worse, it has a documented failure mode with a name.** Lauren Edelman's work on **legal endogeneity** (*Working Law*): organisations construct grievance procedures and compliance structures that courts subsequently defer to as evidence of compliance, while the procedures produce no substantive change. A measured contestation surface becomes a *compliance metric*. "Healthy appeal rates" becomes a target. The grievance channel becomes the proof that grievances are handled.

**S-09 is a recipe for legal endogeneity.** It fails.

## II.2 PRESERVE

What S-09 was pointing at: the classified are not in the contemplative position. That is true and it survives untouched. The error was in the verb. The insight concerns **position**, not data collection — and S-09 converted a claim about position into a proposal for a dataset, which is the contemplative stance recapturing its own critique.

## II.3 ELEVATE — the distinction that was missing

Two things were conflated under "contestation":

- **Token-contestation** — objecting to *the application of a category to a case*. Appeals, disputes, corrections. Operates entirely *inside* the carving. This is what F-08's `R` measures.
- **Type-contestation** — objecting to *the category itself*, with the power to force revision of `κ`. Operates *on* the carving.

Every existing recourse mechanism in algorithmic governance is token-level. Credit disputes, content-moderation appeals, benefits determinations, data-subject rights: all contest whether a case was correctly assigned. **None contests the taxonomy.** There is typically no forum in which "this category should not exist" is a possible speech act with institutional uptake.

This yields a second-order version of F-08, which is what Habermas's own architecture already contains — discourse *about* norms as against action *under* norms:

> **Level-1 colonization:** decisions become unarguable. **Level-2 colonization:** the categories become unarguable — not because they are defended, but because no forum exists in which category-revision is a possible move.

AI systems are paradigmatically level-2. An ontology is fixed in a schema or a label definition, and the affected population has no channel through which to raise a validity claim about the schema. This is F-10.

## II.4 What this does to Lukács

The Lukácsian point is not "gather data from the dominated." The contemplative stance is defined by the **separation of knowing from doing** in the face of regularity taken as given. The exit is therefore not better data and not better reflexivity. It is:

> **Who holds revisionary authority over `κ`.**

This is a *constitutional* criterion, not a methodological one. It cannot be satisfied by any modelling practice, because it is not about modelling.

**Honest residue, recorded rather than resolved:** an analyst who *describes* the distribution of revisionary authority is still an analyst, and the description is available to those holding the authority for use in legitimation. There is no formalism-side escape from Lukács. What Cycle 2 produces is not an escape but a **criterion for when a computational practice is less reified rather than more — and that criterion is constitutional rather than epistemic.**

**Outcome: B (differentiation) + C (model-space transformation).** K-006 moves from `unresolved` to `partially resolved with a permanent residue`. The question "how can a modeller avoid reification?" is replaced by "who can revise the categories, and what would it take to give that power to those the categories bind?"

## II.5 Convergence check

This result and `survivors.md` S-06 were reached independently — one from Lukács via Edelman, one from the measure-theoretic preconditions of FEP. They arrive at the same place: **the unrevisable `Ω*` and the absence of type-contestation are one fact seen from two sides.** Formal closure of the state space and political unaccountability of the carving are the same condition described in two vocabularies.

Termination condition CONVERGENCE met for this thread.

# PART III — Q-05: what distinguishes domination from ordinary asymmetric coupling?

The unresolved normative core. Four candidate answers were constructed and attacked.

## III.1 Candidates and their destruction

**(1) Whose free energy is minimised at whose expense.** A welfare criterion imported wholesale from outside; the formalism carries none of it. Too broad (all parasitism becomes domination) and misses non-zero-sum domination. **Fails.**

**(2) S-07 clause (iii): the residue is rendered nonexistent rather than unrecorded.** Still purely descriptive. Why is administrative nonexistence a wrong? The answer requires a prior commitment the formalism does not contain. **Fails as a source of normativity; survives as a diagnostic once normativity is supplied.**

**(3) Habermas's discourse principle: coordination by means that could not be agreed to by those subject to them under conditions of free discourse.** Genuinely normative, genuinely counterfactual, and *not derivable from any dynamics whatsoever*. **Survives — as an external commitment, which is what it always was.**

**(4) The type-contestation criterion from Part II.** Structural, observable, normatively loaded. **Attacked and damaged:** absence of revisionary authority also describes children, patients under anaesthesia, and everyone with respect to physical law. Authority can be formally present and substantively empty — Edelman again. And it encodes a liberal-proceduralist norm while presenting itself as a structural feature. **Survives only as an operationalisation of (3), not as an independent source of normativity.**

## III.2 The answer

None of the four generates normativity from the formalism, and the pattern of failure is the result:

> **The normative content of a free-energy description is located entirely in the choice of the partition.**

Draw the Markov blanket around the plantation: a homeostatic system minimising free energy, persisting, well-adapted. Draw it around the enslaved person: a system under catastrophic and sustained surprise. **Both descriptions are formally correct. The formalism is indifferent between them.**

The framework does not naturalise domination by endorsing it. It naturalises domination **by making the partition look like a discovery rather than a decision.** The normative act is complete before the first equation is written, and the mathematics that follows is innocent because the guilt was discharged upstream, in a step that presents itself as setup.

## III.3 The four-way convergence

This is Cycle 2's central product, and it was reached from four technically distinct objects with different mathematical roles:

| route | object | what is declared |
|---|---|---|
| S-06 (Cycle 1, FEP preconditions) | the measurable space `Ω` | what can be a state |
| Q-03 / F-06 (Part I) | the cover `𝓜` | which questions are jointly askable |
| K-006 / F-10 (Part II) | the carving `κ` | how situations become cases |
| Q-05 (Part III) | the partition `(μ, s, a, η)` | which system is the one that persists |

Each is declared **before** the formalism can begin; is **unrevisable by** any evidence the formalism generates; carries the **normative and ideological** content; and is **structurally invisible** because it presents as a technical convention rather than as a claim.

> **Husserl's *Substruktion* is not a one-time historical error committed by Galileo. It is the recurring structural moment of every formalisation, and it is locatable by a determinate procedure: find the objects that must exist before the first equation can be written.**

This yields a critique procedure applicable to *any* formal social model, not only to FEP:

1. Enumerate what had to be declared before modelling could begin.
2. Identify who declared it and under what authority.
3. Ask what evidence generated by the model could revise it. (Usually: none.)
4. Ask whether it is contestable by those it binds. (Usually: no.)
5. Ask what has no place in it, and where that residue goes.

It is immanent in the strict sense — it uses only the model's own requirement that it declare a frame. It requires no external standpoint and no rival model.

## III.4 Self-destruction of the convergence

Required, since the finding is the process's most attractive and therefore most suspect.

**Objection (from MC-01):** four "independent" routes generated by one correlated process may converge because the process prefers a single punchline, not because the world does.

**Partial answer:** the four objects are technically distinct — a measurable space, a family of subsets, a function, a partition — with different mathematical roles and different failure modes. The convergence is at the level of *epistemic position* (prior to, and unrevisable by), not of content. That is a weaker and more defensible claim than "they are the same thing."

**Unanswerable part:** the process declared, in its own prompt, which frameworks would be compared and in what vocabulary. The finding that the frame declaration is where the action is *is this process's frame declaration becoming visible to itself*. That is not a flourish. It is the same structure as MC-08 and it is not neutralised by being noticed.

## III.5 Q-05 status

**Answered, negatively and usefully.** No formalism can carry normative content. But every formalism has exactly one place where normative content must be supplied, and that place is identifiable in advance by a determinate procedure. **Critique can therefore be targeted rather than general.**

Moved from `questions.md` to `survivors.md` S-12.

# PART IV — the missing objection (MC-04), and the inversion it produces

`meta_critique.md` MC-04 recorded that no agent was constructed to make the strongest Friston-aligned reply. It is owed, and it turns out to be the most productive move in the cycle.

## IV.1 The defence

> *The state space is a description chosen by the modeller for a purpose. FEP is a modelling principle, not a metaphysics. Nobody claims `Ω*` exists. S-06 attacks a straw metaphysics.*

## IV.2 The reply

**The defence concedes S-06's actual claim.** If `Ω` is the modeller's choice, then results are relative to that choice; the choice is normatively loaded (III.2); it is unrevisable by the model's own evidence; and no FEP-derived social claim can be asserted as a finding about the world rather than about the modeller's frame.

> **You can have the principle or the sociology. Not both.** The defence preserves FEP's metaphysical innocence at the exact cost of its social-explanatory ambition.

What remains permitted: intra-epochal modelling under a declared frame. What is forbidden: the move from *"under this declared partition, X persists by minimising surprise"* to *"X is a free-energy-minimising agent."* The first is a modelling result. The second is a Friston blanket.

## IV.3 A second missing objection, and the strengthening of S-02

Also never made in Cycle 1: **does hierarchical predictive processing rescue the fixed-evidence-functional problem?** At a higher level, a lower level's precisions and priors are set from above — so the lower level's evaluative weighting *is* a variable. Does that give FEP criterion-change after all?

**No, and the reason is exact:** the hierarchy modulates lower-level precision, but the whole hierarchy is scored by one free energy. **The hierarchy pushes the fixed functional upward; it does not eliminate it.** There is no level at which the top-level functional is revised, because there is no level above it. Hegel's claim concerns precisely the top — the criterion the whole shape of consciousness employs.

S-02 survives an attack it had not faced. But the attack leaves a residue that is more valuable than the survival:

> **Heteronomous criterion revision** — a criterion revised from above or outside — is available in FEP at every non-top level.
> **Autonomous criterion revision** — a criterion transformed by the failure of the shape that employs it — is available at no level.

## IV.4 The inversion

That distinction is not a technicality. It is political.

> **A subject whose criteria are revised from above is the colonised subject.** FEP models this natively, precisely, and well. It models its overcoming not at all.

And the same conclusion arrives from Friston's own central concept. **Self-evidencing** — a system acts so as to gather evidence for the model that is itself — is self-relating. But it is *conservative* self-relation: persist as what you are. Hegel's self-relating negativity is the destruction of what a thing is *through the working-out of its own determination*.

> **Self-evidencing is the formal opposite of self-relating negativity.** One preserves identity through change; the other destroys identity through its own determinacy. They are not distant cousins. They are structurally inverse, and they have been mistaken for each other because both are described as "self-relating."

This makes the source paper's worry about naturalising domination precise rather than rhetorical, and then inverts it:

> **FEP is the right formalism for domination and structurally incapable of representing emancipation.** Not by accident of vocabulary — by the architecture of a fixed top-level functional plus self-evidencing.

**The constructive consequence.** The framework is preserved by being turned into the description of its opposite. FEP is not a candidate foundation for critical social theory. It is a candidate **formal model of heteronomy** — of what critical theory opposes. Used that way it is precise, mathematically respectable, and immune to the charge of naturalisation, because naturalisation is only a danger when the formalism is offered as a description of the good order.

This is outcome A applied to the entire programme, in a form the source paper did not consider and would probably not have welcomed: **not FEP as the science of society, but FEP as the exact mathematics of the administered life.**

# PART V — self-application (available only because of Part I)

Part I produced a prediction before this section was written: *coarsening the cover drives the contextuality obstruction to zero while the loss relocates into residual-category load.* The prediction can be tested on this document.

**This process is a single context.** The protocol declared, before any agent spoke, that Husserl, Hegel, Habermas, Lukács, Scott, Bowker & Star, Hacking, Viveiros de Castro and Friston would be compared in one vocabulary — claims, mechanisms, falsification conditions, formalisations. All questions were declared jointly askable.

**Prediction 1: contextuality reads zero.** Confirmed. Cycle 1 achieved determinate adjudication on nearly every proposition. Ten disciplines, one document, agreement or clean disagreement throughout, no obstruction anywhere. By I.1 this is what a coarsened cover produces *by construction*, and it is therefore **not evidence that the frameworks are commensurable.**

**Prediction 2: the loss relocates into residual categories.** Confirmed, and it was recorded before the prediction existed. `meta_critique.md` MC-08: Viveiros de Castro appears as an objection to a formalism (which is not what controlled equivocation is); Adorno appears as one-line ammunition; Husserl's positive phenomenology sits in a column headed `U`, untranslatable. Those are the residual categories of this document's carving, and by F-05's own logic their load is the measure of the carving's failure.

**Prediction 3: no non-isomorphic ontology can be argued *from*.** Confirmed by MC-01, and now explained rather than merely confessed. It is not a limitation of effort. **A single-context process cannot exhibit contextuality**, and arguing from a genuinely different `Ω` would require a second context that the protocol foreclosed at the moment of declaration.

The theory predicts its own blind spot, locates it, and cannot repair it. That is the strongest available evidence for the theory and the strongest available evidence against the process — **and the two are the same observation**, which is the only genuinely dialectical result in either cycle.
